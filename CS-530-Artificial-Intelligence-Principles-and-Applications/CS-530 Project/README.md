<h1 align="center"> AI Applications and Responsible Software Development</h1>

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, the following objectives are demonstrated:

- Evaluate common uses and applications of artificial intelligence (AI) across various disciplines
- Apply prompt engineering in the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools
- Assess the ethical implications of artificial intelligence (AI) systems and technologies

## Module Overview

This module integrates the concepts explored throughout the course by applying artificial intelligence to real-world software development scenarios. Students evaluate AI-generated code, refine prompts using prompt engineering techniques, and examine the ethical, legal, and technical considerations involved in deploying AI-powered development tools. These activities reinforce both practical skills and responsible decision making when working with artificial intelligence.

AI coding assistants such as **GitHub Copilot**, **Tabnine**, **Amazon CodeWhisperer**, and other enterprise AI solutions have become widely adopted across industries including technology, healthcare, finance, education, manufacturing, and entertainment. These tools assist developers by generating code, suggesting improvements, automating repetitive tasks, and increasing development efficiency. While AI can significantly accelerate software development, its outputs must still be evaluated for correctness, security, maintainability, and compliance with project requirements.

Effective use of AI requires more than technical knowledge. Developers must also consider ethical issues such as bias, privacy, transparency, intellectual property, and security when integrating AI into software engineering workflows. Applying prompt engineering strategies and critically evaluating AI-generated results helps ensure that AI serves as a reliable assistant rather than an unquestioned source of information.

Throughout this module, you will apply the knowledge gained throughout the course to evaluate AI tools in practical software development environments. By combining technical expertise with ethical awareness, you will develop the skills needed to responsibly implement artificial intelligence within modern software engineering practices.
