## Module Overview

Artificial intelligence models learn by identifying patterns within large datasets during the training process. Although developers do not directly control the values that a model learns, they do determine several important **hyperparameters** before training begins. These settings influence how efficiently a model learns, how well it generalizes to new data, and how accurately it performs when generating code or responding to user prompts.

Unlike model parameters, which are adjusted automatically during training, hyperparameters remain fixed until they are intentionally changed by the developer. Common hyperparameters include the **learning rate**, **batch size**, **number of training epochs**, and aspects of the model's architecture. Selecting appropriate values helps prevent problems such as overfitting, underfitting, slow convergence, and unstable training.

For large language models (LLMs), hyperparameter tuning is especially important because these models contain billions of parameters and are trained using enormous datasets. Even small adjustments to hyperparameters can significantly affect training time, model accuracy, computational cost, and the quality of AI-generated responses. Effective tuning allows models to produce more reliable, consistent, and accurate outputs while improving their ability to perform well on previously unseen tasks.

Throughout this module, you will examine how hyperparameters influence AI model training and explore their impact on the quality of AI-generated code. Understanding these concepts provides valuable insight into the strengths, limitations, and overall performance of AI-powered software development tools.
