<h1 align="center"> Ethical AI and Responsible Prompt Engineering</h1>

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, the following objectives are demonstrated:

- Examine ethical considerations surrounding AI implementation in various domains
- Analyze bias in AI systems and their implications for decision making
- Evaluate ethical frameworks that guide responsible AI development

## Module Overview

As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly involved in decision making, content generation, and software development, ethical considerations have become just as important as technical capabilities. Prompt engineering not only influences the quality of AI-generated responses but also plays a significant role in promoting fairness, transparency, and accountability. Understanding how prompts affect AI behavior is essential for developing responsible and trustworthy AI applications.

AI systems are trained on large datasets that may contain historical biases or incomplete information. As a result, AI models can unintentionally generate biased, misleading, or discriminatory outputs even when given neutral prompts. Recognizing these limitations allows developers to identify potential risks and create prompts that reduce bias while improving the reliability of AI-generated content.

Responsible AI development extends beyond identifying bias. Developers must also consider fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, and the potential societal impacts of AI systems. Ethical frameworks provide guidance for building AI applications that respect users, minimize harm, and encourage informed decision making throughout the software development lifecycle.

Throughout this module, you will examine real-world examples of AI bias, evaluate ethical frameworks that support responsible AI development, and explore techniques for designing prompts that encourage fair, accurate, and transparent responses. These concepts help ensure that AI serves as a reliable tool while maintaining ethical standards across a variety of industries and applications.
