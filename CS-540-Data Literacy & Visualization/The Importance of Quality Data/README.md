<h1 align="center"> Data Quality and Decision Making</h1>

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, the following objectives are demonstrated:

- Describe the importance of data in decision making
- Explain the factors that contribute to data quality
- Evaluate the quality of given data
- Verify that data comes from credible sources and is reliable for analysis

## Module Overview

Data serves as the foundation for informed decision making in nearly every industry. Organizations rely on accurate and reliable data to identify trends, evaluate performance, solve problems, and develop effective strategies. However, the value of any analysis depends on the quality of the data being used. Understanding how to assess data quality is an essential skill for making sound, evidence-based decisions.

High-quality data is characterized by attributes such as **accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, validity, and reliability**. Data that is incomplete, outdated, inconsistent, or collected from unreliable sources can produce misleading conclusions and result in poor decisions. Evaluating these characteristics helps determine whether a dataset is appropriate for analysis and whether it can be trusted to support meaningful insights.

Equally important is evaluating the credibility of data sources. Reliable data should come from trustworthy organizations, reputable research, or well-documented collection methods. Understanding where data originates, how it was collected, and whether it has been properly maintained allows analysts to identify potential biases, errors, and limitations before using the information.

Throughout this module, you will develop the skills needed to critically evaluate datasets and determine their suitability for decision making. By learning to identify data quality issues and verify the credibility of information sources, you will build a strong foundation for data analysis and visualization throughout the remainder of the course.
