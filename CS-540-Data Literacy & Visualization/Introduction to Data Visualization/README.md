## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, the following objectives are demonstrated:

- Compare different types of visualizations
- Justify the best visualization choice for a given scenario

## Module Overview

Data visualization transforms raw data into graphical representations that make patterns, trends, and relationships easier to understand. Effective visualizations help individuals and organizations communicate complex information clearly, enabling audiences to quickly interpret data and make informed decisions. Selecting the appropriate visualization is essential for accurately presenting information and avoiding misinterpretation.

Different visualization types are designed for different kinds of data and analytical goals. **Bar charts** are effective for comparing categories, **line graphs** illustrate trends over time, **scatterplots** reveal relationships between variables, and **pie charts** display proportions of a whole. More advanced visualizations, including **heatmaps**, **dashboards**, and other interactive displays, provide additional ways to analyze large or complex datasets.

Choosing the best visualization requires understanding both the structure of the data and the purpose of the analysis. Factors such as data type, audience, clarity, and readability all influence which chart is most appropriate. Selecting an unsuitable visualization can obscure important insights, misrepresent relationships, or lead to incorrect conclusions.

Throughout this module, you will compare common visualization techniques and learn how to select the most effective chart for a given dataset and analytical objective. These skills provide the foundation for creating visualizations that communicate data accurately, clearly, and effectively in professional settings.
