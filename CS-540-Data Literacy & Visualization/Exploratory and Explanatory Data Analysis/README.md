<h1 align="center">Exploratory and Explanatory Data Analysis</h1>
Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, the following objectives are demonstrated:

Perform exploratory data analysis
Select visualizations that best support the central message of a data-driven narrative
Differentiate between exploratory and explanatory analysis
Collect relevant data for a given scenario
Module Overview

In this module, you will take a deeper dive into how data analysis informs decision making by learning to distinguish between exploratory and explanatory approaches. Exploratory analysis allows you to uncover trends, patterns, and anomalies without knowing exactly what you will find. In contrast, explanatory analysis is used to communicate insights with a clear narrative once you have discovered what matters. Both are essential to the data storytelling process, and mastering their differences is crucial in applying the right strategy at the right time.

You will practice performing exploratory data analysis (EDA) to uncover insights from unfamiliar data sets, then shift toward selecting the best visualizations to highlight your findings through a focused explanatory lens. This work builds directly toward the course competencies of gathering relevant data and crafting compelling data-driven narratives. These are skills that support effective organizational decision making in any field.

Key questions addressed in this module include:

What is the difference between discovering insights and communicating them?
How can data visuals help frame a strong message?
How do you know when analysis is ready to shift from exploration to explanation?

In this module's assignment, you will practice both types of analysis and evaluate your visualization choices to ensure your message is clear and targeted. Understanding these approaches will help you design visual stories that not only inform but also influence decision making.
